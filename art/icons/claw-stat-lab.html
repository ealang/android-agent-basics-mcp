The 144 px view is the 24 dp box at 6×. Drag the claw there to move it; drag the phone to move it (untick "phone centred" first, otherwise the frame follows the phone and nothing appears to move). Rotation and scale act about the claw's wrist. The shape is built with real polygon booleans in the page: the upper jaw in front, a gap cut out of the phone around it, the lower jaw cut by the phone's outline. The XML box is the finished VectorDrawable, ready to drop in as ic_stat_claw.xml. To restore a saved state, paste its parameter block over the text box and press "Load parameters" (the centred block on its own works too). Style, per shape: "white fill, black outline" is the solid shape with the gap cut out of whatever is behind it; "white outline, black fill" is a ring of the line width with no gap, the black fill being the separation; the third keeps the ring and also cuts the gap, the double edge. Overlaps keep their meaning whichever style is chosen: the upper jaw sits in front of the phone, the phone hides the lower jaw. A hollow phone's speaker slit turns white. Untick "arm" for a floating claw: "back cut" moves the claw's back end forward along the body (0 is the stock outline, where the arm joined) and "back curl" rounds that end off, the two edges curling in along their own tangents until they meet; at 0 the back is a flat cut. "body length" squashes everything behind the hinge towards it, the way jaw length stretches everything in front, so the body can be made as short as the box needs with its curves intact; the cut is taken before the squash.
